Privacy Policy

Welcome to Chaithanya Career Academy. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website and use our mock test services.

When you register on our website to take mock tests, we may collect basic personal information such as your Name, Email Address, and Password. We also collect data regarding your test performance and scores.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others. Your password is securely encrypted in our database. We only share information if required by law.
